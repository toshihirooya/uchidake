Uchidake Camera
Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026

Uchidake Camera (“the app”) turns a spare iPhone into a monitor camera for inside your home. The app's video and audio travel directly between devices in your home and are never sent to a server run by the developer or anyone else. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Video and audio

  • Video and sound captured by the camera device, and your voice when you talk from a viewing device, are encrypted and sent directly between devices in your home that are connected over the same Wi-Fi or a similar local link.
  • They never pass through any server run by the developer or a third party. The developer cannot see, hear, or store them.
  • The camera device does not store any video or audio.
  • Photos and recordings you take while watching are saved only to the Photos app on the viewing device.

2. Information stored only on your device

  • The key that links your devices: a random key created on your first device. It is stored in the device's keychain and is not transferred to other devices through iCloud or backups. It reaches another device only when you scan its QR code.
  • The camera's name and how you last used the app (whether the device was a camera), so the next launch is quicker.
  • A copy of your Family Plan status, so the plan keeps working when the device is offline.

None of this is sent to the developer. It is removed when you delete the app (the keychain key can also be erased with “Start setup over” in the app).

3. Information visible on your home network

So that viewing devices can find it, a camera device announces itself on your home network (Bonjour). This announcement is not encrypted and may be visible to others on the same network. It includes the camera's name, a tag that tells whether devices belong to the same group, and whether the Family Plan is active. It never includes video, audio, or the key.

4. Family Plan (in-app purchase)

  • The app uses RevenueCat, Inc.'s service (the RevenueCat SDK) to process purchases and restores of the Family Plan and to check whether it is active.
  • For this, your purchase history (which product was purchased and when, expiry dates, and so on) and an anonymous ID that RevenueCat creates for each device are sent to RevenueCat's servers. No information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, or Apple Account details, is sent.
  • This information is used only to provide the Family Plan, never for advertising or tracking.
  • For how RevenueCat handles this information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Payments are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your credit card or other payment details.

5. Device features the app uses

  • Camera: to capture video when the device is a camera, and to scan QR codes when adding a device.
  • Microphone: to pick up sound while someone is watching a camera, and to send your voice while you hold “Hold to talk” on a viewing device.
  • Local network: to find and connect the cameras and viewing devices in your home.
  • Add to Photos: to save photos and recordings taken while watching.

Each is used only if you allow it in the iOS permission prompt.

6. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app does not collect usage analytics, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children. When you use it to watch over children or pets, video and audio stay between the devices in your home.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.

9. Contact

For privacy questions about the app, contact contact@toshihirooya.com.
