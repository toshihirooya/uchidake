Uchidake Camera

Turn a spare iPhone into a home monitor camera and check on your pets or kids from your iPhone or iPad. Video and audio never go through a server — devices in your home talk to each other directly.

What you need

  • Two devices running iOS / iPadOS 17 or later (an iPhone to use as the camera, and an iPhone or iPad to watch from)
  • Both devices on the same Wi-Fi (without Wi-Fi, nearby devices can sometimes connect directly)

Getting started

  1. Open the app on the first device and tap “Start fresh”. A QR code appears.
  2. Open the app on the second device and use “Scan QR code” to scan it.
  3. On the device you want to leave in place, tap “Start camera”. On the device in your hand, tap “Find cameras”. Keep the camera device charging with the app open.

FAQ

The camera doesn't show up, or won't connect
Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, and that Settings › Uchidake Camera › Local Network is turned on for both. If you used “Start setup over” on one device, scan the QR code again so both share the same key.

Can I watch when I'm away from home?
No. The app only connects devices inside your home, so your video never goes out to the internet.

Does it work in the dark?
It uses low-light boost on devices that support it, but there is no infrared night vision, so a completely dark room won't be visible.

What can I do for free?
One camera and one viewer, with every feature, with no time limit. The Family Plan lets up to four devices watch one camera at the same time, and lets you run as many cameras as you like.

I bought the Family Plan but it isn't active
The Family Plan is activated on the device you use as the camera. On that device, sign in with the Apple Account that made the purchase (or a Family Sharing member), open the Family Plan screen, and tap “Restore Purchases”.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.
