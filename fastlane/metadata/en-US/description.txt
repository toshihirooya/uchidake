Turn a spare iPhone into a monitor camera for your home.
Check on your pets while they're home alone, or on your kids sleeping in another room, from your iPhone or iPad.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN — NO SERVERS
The camera and the devices you watch from connect directly over your home Wi-Fi. Video and audio are never sent to any server, including ours, and there's no account to create. Everything between your devices is encrypted.

SET UP WITH A QR CODE
Start on your first device, scan its QR code with the second, and you're done. Only devices in the same group can connect.

FEATURES
• Live video
• Listen to sound around the camera — even after you close the app
• Hold to talk
• Switch between the front and back cameras
• Take photos and record video while watching (saved to Photos on the viewing device)
• Low battery warning for the camera

HARD TO USE FOR HIDDEN RECORDING
The camera screen always shows that it's running and how many devices are watching.

EVERY FEATURE, FREE
One camera and one viewer get every feature, with no time limit.

FAMILY PLAN (IN-APP PURCHASE)
• Up to four devices can watch one camera at the same time
• Run as many cameras as you like
• Choose monthly (first 7 days free), yearly, or a one-time purchase — all include the same features
• The Family Plan is activated on the device you use as the camera. Family Sharing is supported.

PLEASE NOTE
• For use inside your home only. You can't watch from outside (a different network).
• There is no infrared night vision, so a completely dark room won't be visible.
• Keep the camera device charging with the app open.
• This is not a medical device. Don't rely on this app alone for the safety of infants.

ABOUT AUTO-RENEWING SUBSCRIPTIONS
• Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically and are charged to your Apple Account unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
• You can cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
• Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.
